Privacy Policy

Waikato Interlocks · Last updated 10 November 2026

Who we are

Waikato Interlocks installs and services alcohol interlocks in the Waikato. Contact us at waikatointerlocks@gmail.com or 027 264 6986.

What we collect

Your name, mobile number, email address, vehicle details, interlock install date, subsidy status, your bookings and payments, and any NZTA approval number or licence photo you give us for a removal.

Why

To book and carry out your interlock servicing, work out what you pay, send you reminders and service messages, and meet our obligations as an interlock service provider.

Signing in

If you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email address from Google to confirm who you are. We don't get access to anything else in your Google account. If you sign in with a text code, we use your mobile number only to send that code.

Who sees it

Only Waikato Interlocks staff. We share information with Dräger and NZTA where the interlock programme requires it. We don't sell your information or use it for advertising.

Where it's kept

In a secured database hosted in Australia. Text messages are sent through a New Zealand messaging provider.

How long

For as long as you're a customer, and afterwards for as long as tax and programme records require.

Your rights

You can ask to see or correct your information at any time by contacting us. If you're not happy with our response, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner at privacy.org.nz.

Messages

We send texts and notifications about your bookings. You can turn reminders off by telling us.
